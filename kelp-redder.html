Meet Kelp Redder

Magic, technology, friendship and a remarkable number of terrible ideas.

THE KELP REDDER ADVENTURES

Small problems. Very large consequences.

Eleven-year-old elf Kelp Redder lives in a world where magic sits comfortably beside phones, routers and the internet. With his human best friend Gall Tyme, every attempt to fix a small problem has a habit of becoming a much larger adventure.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Kelp's Rules

  1. Do not panic.
  2. Do not touch mysterious glowing things unless absolutely necessary.
  3. If Gall says it is a bad idea, consider listening.
  4. Mugwaps are not pets.
  5. Eat cake when possible.

THE TWO RESPONSIBLE FOR MOST OF THIS

Meet Kelp and Gall

Best friends, reluctant problem-solvers and frequent witnesses to plans becoming much worse.

ELF · AGE 11

Kelp Redder

Magical, confident and usually responsible for the sentence, “What did you do?” Kelp believes most problems can be improved with magic. The evidence does not support this.

HUMAN · AGE 11

Gall Tyme

Practical, good with technology and sensible enough to recognise a terrible idea. Unfortunately, recognising one and stopping Kelp from doing it are very different skills.
