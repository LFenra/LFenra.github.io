About L. Fenra

L. Fenra lives in Ayrshire, Scotland, with his wife, son and their dachshund, Milo. Before turning to writing, he worked as both a blacksmith and a joiner, two trades that taught him a fair bit about patience, problem-solving and making things from scratch.

Now he no longer builds things in the real world, he has turned that same instinct and joy for creating towards fiction, building Prima Volta's world one case at a time — usually with Milo somewhere nearby, supervising.

“Magic does malfunction. People just do it more often.”— Prima Volta
