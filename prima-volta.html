THE PRIMA VOLTA SERIES

Some crimes leave impossible evidence

Prima Volta investigates magical incidents that do not behave like ordinary crimes: dangerous relics, altered memories, discarded histories and cases where cause and effect refuse to agree. She trusts evidence first, magic second, and patience only when absolutely necessary.

“Magic does malfunction. People just do it more often.”PRIMA VOLTA

THE INVESTIGATOR

Prima Volta

Arcane investigator, reluctant optimist and persistent enemy of convenient explanations.

ARCANE INVESTIGATOR

Follow the evidence

Prima is methodical when the case permits it and dangerous when it does not. She reads magical traces the way another detective reads fingerprints, with a dry sense of humour and very little tolerance for anyone who assumes magic is an excuse for sloppy thinking.
